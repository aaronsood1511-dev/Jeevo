Our story

A family business,
carried forward.

Carrying forward the legacy of Mike Chatterjee, his wife Mrs. Promila Chatterjee continues the journey he began: creating travel that goes far beyond sightseeing and the logistics of getting from one place to another.

Mike believed every journey should be more than a visit to a destination. It should be a story worth telling, a memory worth cherishing, and a chance to form real connections with the people, cultures and places met along the way.

That philosophy is still the whole of it. We curate journeys that bring you closer to the soul of a place — ancient temples, food cooked the way it has always been cooked, and time spent with the communities that give each region its character.

What sets the trips apart is the care behind the detail. Every traveller is looked after as a guest of the family, not as a booking reference.

How a trip comes together

Planned in a day.
Looked after throughout.

01Tell us

Where, when, how many, and roughly how you want it to feel. Arjun, our planning assistant at jeevo.co.in, drafts a day-by-day outline in about a minute.

02We shape it

A consultant takes that draft and makes it real — the right order, sensible drives, hotels we trust, and the mornings worth getting up for.

03We run it

Our own drivers and guides on the ground, reachable at any hour. If something changes, we change with it.
