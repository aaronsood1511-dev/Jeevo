About Us

Jeevo — Travel That Becomes Part of Your Story

Carrying forward the legacy of Mike Chatterjee, his wife, Mrs. Promila Chatterjee, continues the journey he so passionately began: creating travel experiences that go far beyond sightseeing and the simple logistics of getting from one place to another.

Mike believed that every journey should be more than a visit to a destination. It should be a story worth telling, a memory worth cherishing, and an opportunity to form meaningful connections with the people, cultures, and places encountered along the way.

That philosophy remains at the heart of everything we do. We thoughtfully curate immersive travel experiences that bring you closer to the soul of a destination, whether through exploring ancient temples, discovering authentic local cuisine, or sharing moments with the communities that give each place its unique character.

Those journeys run from the deeply spiritual to the unmistakably historical. A morning at the Harmandir Sahib as the first prayers carry across the water, where anyone who walks in is fed and nobody is asked who they are. An afternoon in the marble shade of the Taj Mahal, built by an emperor for one woman. India holds both without the slightest contradiction, and we build around whichever one you came for — as a pilgrim, as a student of history, or simply as someone curious enough to look.

What truly sets our journeys apart is the care behind every detail. With warmth, personal attention, and genuine hospitality, we ensure that every traveller feels welcomed, valued, and connected, not merely as a visitor, but as a cherished member of our extended family.

This is more than a travel company. It is a heartfelt continuation of Mike's vision. In every thoughtfully crafted itinerary, every shared smile, and every unexpected moment of discovery, his spirit lives on, inspiring us to create journeys that enrich, connect, and remain with you long after you return home.
